# United States search plan

Primary market: United States. Primary language: English. Primary query: gamepad tester.
Ranking destination: the existing homepage. Keep the tool available worldwide.

## Measurement

- Search Console Performance: Web search, Country = United States, Query = exact gamepad tester, Page = homepage.
- Review impressions, clicks, CTR and average position weekly; compare 28-day periods once enough data exists.
- Track related non-branded queries separately: controller tester, online gamepad test, stick drift test. Absence of a query in Search Console is not proof of a specific ranking.
- In SearchAtlas set the database to United States and record its date, volume estimate, difficulty and competing URLs. No US volume or difficulty estimate has been verified yet.

## Next evidence to collect

Real controller tests with model, firmware, OS, browser, connection, screenshots and limitations. Begin with available Xbox or DualSense hardware; do not invent access to devices or test results.

## Promotion

Create a short reproducible demonstration and contribute useful findings to relevant gaming or repair communities where permitted. Seek editorial mentions rather than buying links. No outreach has been sent.

## Technical scope

The homepage title, opening description, WebSite schema and Open Graph branding identify the tool and brand. Site-name markup expresses a preference; Google can choose a different name. en_US social metadata and en-US schema describe content, not a Google country-targeting switch. Do not add duplicate /us pages or hreflang without real localized alternatives.

After deployment, confirm the homepage serves the new metadata and allow Google to recrawl. The successful sitemap does not need repeated resubmission for this copy change. Indexing and ranking are separate; there is no promised ranking or deadline.
